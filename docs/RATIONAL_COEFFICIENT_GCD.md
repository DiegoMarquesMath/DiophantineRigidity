# From rational coefficient coprimality to bounded integer W_t

The coefficient family generates the unit ideal in Q[X]:
    (A, V_0, ..., V_k) = Q[X].
This is an exact algebraic characterization of gcd 1 in a PID.

The new mathematical assertion is that no complex root of A can
annihilate every V_j. The proof uses the ring homomorphism
eval_ρ : Q[X] -> C and the inclusion of ideals in its kernel.

A follow-up declaration combines this condition with bounded
root avoidance to construct one integral W_t, t<=kD,
coprime to A over C[X], rather than *assuming* rootwise nonvanishing.

This still does not prove that the integral resultant with nominal
degree e is nonzero; Sylvester kernel rigidity is handled separately.
The full p-adic projective-content estimate remains open.
